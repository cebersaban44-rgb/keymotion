Every Keymotion version comes with release notes that say what changed.

## Where to find them

- **On GitHub.** The [Releases page](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/releases) has the notes for each version, next to its installer and the installer's SHA-256 checksum.
- **In Keymotion.** When Keymotion finds a new version, click **What's new** in **Settings**, under **About**, or **Install now** in the strip above the page. The update window shows the notes under **What's new**, in the app's language when the release has notes in that language. Otherwise it shows a **Release notes on GitHub** link.
- **The full history.** All versions are listed in [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) on GitHub.

## Keymotion 0.1.0

The first release. It supports the AJAZZ AK980 PRO on Windows.

- **Connection.** Finds the keyboard over the USB cable, the 2.4 GHz receiver and Bluetooth. Settings need the USB cable; Bluetooth shows the battery level.
- **Overview.** The whole keyboard with every key's letters, what the screen shows now, and a tile for each tool.
- **Lighting.** The 20 built-in effects, per-key color with a brush and selection tools, and color calibration.
- **Key mapping and macros.** Base and Fn layers, a reset to the factory layout, and macros you record by pressing keys.
- **Screen.** Images, GIFs, videos, text cards and slideshows for the 240×135 screen, a library, and a way back to the factory animation. Keymotion also has an animation of its own.
- **Live screen.** Cards that update by themselves: the song that is playing, the focus timer and upcoming meetings, within a daily upload limit.
- **Tools.** A focus timer, reminders, calendar cards from Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars, and system gauges on the keys.
- **In the background.** Closing the window keeps Keymotion running next to the clock, and it can start with Windows.
- **Updates and problem reports.** Keymotion checks GitHub for new versions and installs only when you choose. Problems are reported on GitHub, by you.
- **Installer.** For your Windows account only, in English and Turkish, with no administrator rights needed.
- Turkish and English interface, light and dark theme.

Keymotion 0.1.0 isn't code-signed yet. See the [code signing policy](/keymotion/about/code-signing/).
