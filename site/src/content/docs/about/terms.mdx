Keymotion is a free desktop app for Windows that controls your keyboard: its lighting, key mapping, macros, screen and clock. These terms say what you can expect from it and what it expects from you. They were last changed on 16 November 2026.

## Free and open source

- Keymotion costs nothing. It has no account, no ads and no paid version.
- Its source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion). You can use, copy, change and share it under the [MIT license](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/blob/main/LICENSE-MIT) or the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/blob/main/LICENSE-APACHE), whichever you prefer. Those licenses are the legal terms for the software itself; this page doesn't change them.
- You can download it from GitHub or the Microsoft Store. Both are the same app. The Microsoft Store's own terms also apply to a copy from the Store.

## No warranty

Keymotion is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as both licenses say. Nobody promises that it works with your keyboard or for your purpose, and nobody is liable for damage that comes from using it.

Keymotion writes to the keyboard's own memory when you save lighting, key mapping, macros or a picture. The way it talks to these keyboards comes from measuring them, not from documents the makers published. If something goes wrong, [If something goes wrong](/keymotion/help/recovery/) explains how to bring the keyboard back to how it was.

## Your keyboard and its maker

Keymotion is an independent project. It isn't made, endorsed, sponsored or supported by AJAZZ, AULA or any other keyboard brand, by the maker of the keyboard's controller chip, or by the authors of the keyboard's official software. Product names are used only to say which hardware Keymotion works with.

## Games

- Keymotion doesn't read or change any game's memory, doesn't send keys to games and loads no drivers. It sees which program is in front, reads your launchers' game lists, and, during a League of Legends match, reads the match data the game itself offers on your computer. [Game session](/keymotion/guide/game-session/) explains how.
- Each game and tournament has its own rules. Macros, even ones stored on the keyboard, can break the rules of competitive games. Following those rules is up to you. If a game's anti-cheat software gives you trouble, turn on **Send nothing to the keyboard while playing** on the **Game session** page.
