## What you can do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Lighting" icon="sun">
    The keyboard's 20 effects, a color for every key, and a live mirror of what the keys show. [Lighting](/keymotion/guide/lighting/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Key mapping" icon="puzzle">
    Change any key on the base and Fn layers: shortcuts, media keys, mouse buttons. [Key mapping](/keymotion/guide/key-mapping/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Macros" icon="random">
    Record a sequence by pressing keys, then edit each step and its timing. [Macros](/keymotion/guide/macros/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Screen studio" icon="window">
    Put images, GIFs, videos and text cards on the 240×135 screen. [Screen](/keymotion/guide/screen/)

  <Card title="Live screen" icon="analytics">
    Cards that update on their own: the song that is playing, your next meeting, the focus timer. [Screen](/keymotion/guide/screen/)

## What works in each connection mode

The switch on the back of the keyboard picks the connection. Settings travel over the USB cable; that is how the keyboard is built.

| Connection | AJAZZ AK980 PRO |
|---|---|
| USB cable | Everything: lighting, keys, macros, the screen and live cards |
| 2.4 GHz receiver | Typing, media keys and the knob |
| Bluetooth | Typing, media keys, the knob and the battery level |
