Almost everything Keymotion changes on the keyboard can be put back from inside the app. This page goes through the common cases, then says where to ask when it doesn't help.

<Aside type="note" title="Needs the USB cable">
Everything on this page that changes the keyboard works only over the USB cable, with the switch on the back of the keyboard in the middle.
</Aside>

## Put the factory screen animation back

The keyboard's screen has two pages. The **Clock page** never changes. The **GIF page** shows the animation the keyboard came with, until you upload something else: every upload replaces it. Press Fn+Home on the keyboard to switch between the two.

Keymotion doesn't come with AJAZZ's animation, but it can keep a copy of it in your library. With that copy, you can put the animation back at any time.

### If your library has a copy

<Steps>

1. Open **Screen** in the sidebar and stay on the **Images** tab.
2. In the **Library**, find **AJAZZ factory animation**. It's marked **Factory**.
3. Click **Restore**, then **Upload** in the window that opens.
4. Keep the cable plugged in until it finishes. It takes about 5 minutes, and the keyboard's screen shows LOADING % meanwhile.

</Steps>

### If there's no copy yet

When there's no copy, the Screen page says "There's no backup of the factory animation."

- **AJAZZ's official driver is installed on this computer.** The page says so and shows **Back up from the official driver**. Click it. Keymotion copies the animation from the driver's files into your library as **AJAZZ factory animation**, and nothing is sent to the keyboard. Then put it back as described above.
- **The driver isn't installed.** The page says that AJAZZ's official driver can restore it. Install the driver, restart Keymotion, and the backup button appears. You can also put the animation back with AJAZZ's driver itself.

If Keymotion says "The official driver's file wasn't found", install the driver, then restart Keymotion.

<Screenshot name="screen-no-backup" alt="The Screen page saying there's no backup of the factory animation, with the Back up from the official driver button" />

<Aside type="caution" title="Make the copy before your first upload">
An upload to the GIF page, or a card from the live screen, replaces the factory animation on the keyboard. While Keymotion has no copy, the upload window warns you, and turning on the live screen asks you first. Without a copy, only AJAZZ's official driver can bring the animation back.
</Aside>

## Use the Keymotion animation instead

Keymotion comes with an animation of its own: the "K" of its logo drawn stroke by stroke, then its name. It's always in the library as **Keymotion animation**, marked **Comes with the app**, and can't be deleted.

<Steps>

1. Open **Screen**, on the **Images** tab.
2. In the **Library**, find **Keymotion animation** and click **Upload**.
3. Click **Upload** again in the window that opens. It takes about a minute and a half.

</Steps>

When there's no copy of the factory animation, the Screen page also offers it: click **Show in library** to go to it.

## Reset key assignments to the factory layout

The keyboard can't report its key mapping, so the **Key mapping** page shows what you last saved from Keymotion.

<Steps>

1. Open **Key mapping**.
2. Choose **Base layer** or **Fn layer**.
3. Click **Reset layer**. If you can't click it, that layer already has the factory layout as far as Keymotion knows.
4. Do the same on the other layer if you changed it too.
5. Click **Save to keyboard**.

</Steps>

To put back a single key, select it on the keyboard drawing and click **Restore default**. On the Fn layer, this also brings back the key's built-in shortcut.

Macros stay on the keyboard until you delete them: open **Macros**, select one, click **Delete macro**, then **Save to keyboard**. Keys assigned to a deleted macro go back to their default.

<Aside type="tip" title="Everything at once">
In **Profiles**, the **Default** profile holds the keyboard's factory lighting and key layout, with no macros. Click **Load to keyboard** to put all of it back in one go. It deletes your macros from the keyboard, and the screen picture doesn't change. See [Profiles](/keymotion/guide/profiles/).
</Aside>

## Turn the lighting back to an effect

Use this after a per-key color design, or when the lights are off.

<Steps>

1. Open **Lighting** and choose the **Effects** tab.
2. Choose an effect. The one the keyboard came with is marked **Default**, and **Restore default effect** sets it with its original settings.
3. Click **Save to keyboard**.

</Steps>

Saving an effect replaces the per-key color design on the keyboard.

<Aside type="note" title="If the lights stay off">
With the **Off** effect saved, Fn+X on the keyboard may not turn the light back on. Choose another effect and save it as above.
</Aside>

If the keys show bars instead of your lighting, system gauges are on. The Lighting page says so. Click **Go to gauges** and turn off **Show on keys**.

## An upload was cut short

During an upload to the screen, keep the cable plugged in. If the cable comes out, or Keymotion closes before the upload finishes, the GIF page holds only part of the picture and may look broken. The Clock page isn't affected.

Keymotion says "The upload stopped before it finished" and remembers it:

- The **Screen** page says "The last upload didn't finish", and the picture is marked **Incomplete** in the library.
- The next time you start Keymotion, a notice with the same title appears at the bottom right of the window.

<Steps>

1. Plug the cable back in and set the switch on the back to the middle.
2. Click **Upload again**, on the Screen page or in the notice.
3. Click **Upload** in the window that opens.

</Steps>

The picture is uploaded again from the beginning. If it's no longer in your library, upload any other picture from the library instead.

<Screenshot name="screen-incomplete-upload" alt="The Screen page saying the last upload didn't finish, with the Upload again button" />

If you quit Keymotion during an upload, it shows "Saving to the keyboard" and closes by itself when the upload finishes. **Quit now** leaves the GIF page incomplete.

## Another program is using the keyboard

The keyboard follows one program's commands at a time. When two programs send commands together, they can mix up, so Keymotion doesn't save anything to the keyboard while one of these is running:

- AJAZZ's official driver: "The AJAZZ driver is running. Close it before saving to the keyboard."
- SignalRGB or OpenRGB, with the same kind of message.

Close that program completely, then click **Save to keyboard** again. Nothing was written, and your edits are still in Keymotion.

When more than one keyboard is connected by USB, Keymotion doesn't save either: "More than one keyboard is connected by USB. Leave only one connected to save."

## Keymotion doesn't find the keyboard

While there's no keyboard, Keymotion shows **Connect your keyboard** and "Looking for the keyboard…".

<Steps>

1. Plug the USB-C cable into the keyboard and into your computer.
2. Set the switch on the back of the keyboard to the middle, the USB position.
3. Keymotion finds the keyboard by itself within a few seconds.

</Steps>

If it still doesn't appear:

- Try another cable or another USB port.
- Check the switch. With the switch on 2.4G or BT, the keyboard works wirelessly and Keymotion can't change its settings. Pages that need the cable say "Your keyboard isn't connected". See [Connection](/keymotion/guide/connection/).
- If Keymotion shows "This keyboard isn't supported yet", it found a keyboard it doesn't know. Nothing is sent to it. See [Supported keyboards](/keymotion/help/keyboards/).
- If Keymotion says "This keyboard's firmware wasn't recognized, so no commands were sent to it", please [report it](#where-to-ask-for-help).

More about the first connection is in [Connecting the keyboard](/keymotion/guide/connecting/).

## Uninstall Keymotion

<Steps>

1. In Windows Settings, open **Apps**, then **Installed apps**.
2. Find **Keymotion**, click the **…** button next to it, and choose **Uninstall**.
3. If Keymotion is open, the uninstaller offers to close it. Click **OK**.
4. To remove your settings and saved data too, tick **Also delete my settings and saved data**. Leave it unticked to keep them for a later install.

</Steps>

Uninstalling also removes Keymotion from the programs that start with Windows, and its name from Windows notifications.

<Aside type="caution" title="Deleting your saved data">
Your saved data includes your picture library, the copy of the factory animation, and Keymotion's only record of your key mapping and macros. If you delete it, you can't restore the factory animation from Keymotion later.
</Aside>

The keyboard keeps its key mapping and macros, lighting, screen picture and clock after you uninstall Keymotion. To clear them before you give the keyboard away, follow the steps above while Keymotion is still installed: reset the key mapping, delete the macros and save, then upload a neutral picture, such as the Keymotion animation.

What Keymotion stores, and where, is in the [privacy policy](/keymotion/about/privacy/).

## Where to ask for help

- **Report a problem.** Open **Settings**. Under **About**, next to **Feedback**, click **Report a problem**. A window shows the details that will be included first. **Report on GitHub** copies them and opens the project's form on GitHub in your browser. Paste them into the form (Ctrl+V) and send it. You need a GitHub account.
- **After an error or a crash.** When something unexpected goes wrong, or Keymotion closed unexpectedly the last time, it offers the same report window.
- **If the window shows "Something went wrong".** Click **Reload**. If it keeps happening, report it.
- **Ask a question.** Use [Questions and help](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/discussions/categories/q-a) on GitHub.
- **A security problem.** Please report it privately with [the private report form](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/security/advisories/new), not in a public report.

Keymotion sends nothing by itself. You see the text first and decide whether to send it. More answers are in the [FAQ](/keymotion/help/faq/).
