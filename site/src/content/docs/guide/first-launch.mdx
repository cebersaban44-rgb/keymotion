The first time Keymotion opens, it looks for your keyboard and takes its language and theme from Windows. You can use part of the app before the keyboard is connected.

## Opening Keymotion

Open **Keymotion** from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut. As the window opens, a short animation draws the Keymotion logo and moves it to the top of the sidebar. It lasts about a second while the app gets ready; click or press a key to end it sooner. With Windows' animation effects turned off, the window simply fades in.

## Language and theme

- **Language.** Keymotion opens in Turkish when Windows' display language is Turkish, and in English otherwise. To change it, open **Settings** and choose **Türkçe** or **English** next to **Language**.
- **Theme.** Keymotion follows Windows' light or dark mode. To keep one of them, open **Settings** and choose **Light** or **Dark** next to **Theme**. **System** follows Windows again.

When the window is narrow, the sidebar shows only icons. Point at an icon to see its name.

## The welcome page

Until a keyboard is found, the **Overview** and **Connection** pages show the welcome page.

<Screenshot name="welcome" alt="The welcome page: the connection steps, a drawing of the switch on the back of the keyboard set to USB, and the tools you can use without a keyboard" />

- **Connect your keyboard** lists three steps: plug in the USB-C cable, set the switch on the back of the keyboard to the middle (USB) position, and wait. Under the steps, **Looking for the keyboard…** shows that Keymotion is searching; it looks every two seconds. The drawing next to the steps shows where the switch should be. [Connecting the keyboard](/keymotion/guide/connecting/) explains the switch.
- **Supported keyboard** names the AJAZZ AK980 PRO. **Have a different keyboard?** opens the list of keyboards on GitHub; [Supported keyboards](/keymotion/help/keyboards/) has the same list.
- **You can also use it without a keyboard** links to the **Focus timer**, **Reminders** and **Screen pictures**, where you can prepare pictures and save them to the library.

As soon as the keyboard is found, the Overview takes the welcome page's place. You don't need to restart anything.

<Aside type="note" title="An independent app">
At the bottom, the welcome page says that Keymotion is an independent app, not affiliated with AJAZZ, and that it writes to the keyboard's permanent memory only with your approval. AJAZZ doesn't make or support Keymotion, so report problems with it to the project on GitHub; [Settings](/keymotion/guide/settings/) shows how.
</Aside>

### A keyboard that isn't supported yet

If you plug in a SONiX or AJAZZ keyboard that Keymotion doesn't support yet, a card with its name appears at the top: **This keyboard isn't supported yet**. **Request support** opens a window with the keyboard's details, which you can send to the project on GitHub. Nothing is written to the keyboard.

## Without the keyboard

- **Overview** and **Connection** show the welcome page.
- **Lighting**, **Key mapping** and **Macros** have a lock in the sidebar and say **Your keyboard isn't connected**. **How to connect** takes you to the welcome page.
- **Screen**, **Profiles**, **Focus timer**, **System gauges**, **Reminders**, **Calendar** and **Settings** open as usual. What they show on the keyboard, such as lights, screen cards and uploads, needs the cable; the rest works in the app.
- The box at the bottom of the sidebar says **Waiting for a keyboard** and **Connect the USB cable**.

## With the keyboard

When the keyboard is connected with the USB cable and its switch is in the middle, Keymotion opens the [Overview](/keymotion/guide/overview/). The box at the bottom of the sidebar shows **AJAZZ AK980 PRO** and **USB cable**, with a green dot.

Over the 2.4 GHz receiver or Bluetooth, the keyboard is found too, but the pages that change its settings stay locked, and the Overview shows **Unlock every feature with the USB cable**. [Connecting the keyboard](/keymotion/guide/connecting/) shows what works over each connection.

When the keyboard connects over USB, Keymotion also sets the clock on the keyboard's screen, at most once a day. You can turn this off on the [Connection](/keymotion/guide/connection/) page.
