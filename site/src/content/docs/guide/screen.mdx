The AK980 PRO has a small 240×135 screen. On the **Screen** page you make a picture for it, keep your pictures in a library, and turn on the live screen, which shows cards for your meetings, reminders, focus timer and music by itself.

The page has two tabs: **Images** for your own pictures and **Live screen** for the cards. Above both, you see what the keyboard's screen shows now.

<Screenshot name="screen-studio" alt="The Screen page on the Images tab, with the keyboard's clock page and GIF page at the top and the three sources below" />

<Aside type="note" title="Uploading needs the USB cable">
To upload, connect the cable and set the switch on the back of the keyboard to the middle. With the 2.4 GHz receiver or Bluetooth you can still make pictures and save them to the library, then upload them later.
</Aside>

## The keyboard's two pages

The screen has two pages:

- **Clock page:** the time and date, drawn by the keyboard itself. It can't be changed and always stays.
- **GIF page:** the picture you upload. It holds one picture at a time, so each upload replaces the one before.

Press **Fn+Home** on the keyboard to switch between the two. When Keymotion sets the keyboard clock, the screen goes back to the clock page.

At the top of the Screen page, the two pages are drawn side by side, with how many uploads you made today and in total.

## Make a picture

Making a picture takes four steps, shown at the top of the studio: **Source**, **Edit**, **Preview** and **Upload**. Until the upload starts, **Cancel** at the top right takes you back to the start and nothing is sent to the keyboard.

<Steps>

1. Open **Screen** in the sidebar and stay on the **Images** tab.
2. Under **What should the screen show?**, choose a source: **Image or video**, **Text card** or **Slideshow**.
3. Edit it (see below), then click **Preview**.
4. Check the preview. **Keyboard view** plays exactly what the keyboard will get. Type a **Name** for it.
5. Click **Upload to keyboard**. To keep it for later instead, click **Save to library**. To change something, click **Back to editing**.
6. The **Upload to the keyboard?** window shows the number of frames, how long the upload takes and which upload of the day this is. Click **Upload**.

</Steps>

While it uploads, a bar shows how far it is and the time left. The keyboard's screen shows LOADING %. When it is done, the studio goes back to the first step and says what was uploaded and how long it took. Every picture you upload is also saved to the library.

A still picture uploads in a few seconds. A long animation takes longer: the screen holds up to 140 frames, and a full 140-frame animation takes about five and a half minutes. Each editor shows the upload time before you start.

<Screenshot name="screen-text-card" alt="The Edit step of a text card, with the Name card template, its text fields and colors" />

### Image or video

Click **Choose file**, or drag a file onto the tile. You can use PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, APNG, MP4 and WebM files. Images and GIFs can be up to 64 MB, videos up to 1 GB. A picture can be up to 8192×8192 pixels and a file up to 3,000 frames.

- Drag the picture to place it, and scroll to zoom. **Placement** offers **Fill**, **Fit** and **Stretch**. **Center** puts it back in the middle.
- For animations and videos, **Start** and **End** pick the part you want, and **Preview position** shows any moment of it.
- **Frame rate** sets how many frames a second are kept. For an animated image, **Original** keeps the file's own timing. Videos and very long animations start at 10 frames a second, so 14 seconds fit in 140 frames. If your selection is longer, the rest is cut and a note says so. Shorten the selection or lower the frame rate.
- **Smooth gradients** reduces color bands in photos and videos.

The colors in the editor are the colors the screen can show.

### Text card

Click **Make a card**, then pick a **Template**: **Name card**, **Quote**, **Scrolling text**, **Mountains** or **Badge**.

- Type your text in the fields beside the preview.
- **Text style** offers **Bold**, **Plain** and **Technical**. You can also set **All caps**, **Text size** and **Alignment**.
- Under **Colors**, pick a color theme or set the text, accent and background colors yourself. **Gradient** blends two background colors.
- **Scrolling text** also has **Speed** and **Frame rate**.

If the text color is too close to the background, a note warns you that it may be hard to read.

### Slideshow

Click **Make a slideshow**. Add pictures with **Add files**, by dragging them onto the preview, or with **Add from library**. A slideshow needs at least two pictures and can have up to 24.

- Each slide has a **Duration** from 1 to 20 seconds. A slide from a file can **Fill** or **Fit** the screen.
- Move slides with **Move up** and **Move down**, and remove one with **Remove slide**.
- **Transition** is **Cut** or **Fade**. A fade takes 0.3 seconds.
- **Frames used** shows how much of the 140 frames the slideshow takes. Every half second of a slide uses one frame. If it goes over 140, shorten the durations, remove a slide or choose **Cut**.

## The library

Under the studio, the **Library** keeps every picture you uploaded or saved. It is the only copy: the keyboard can't give a picture back.

<Screenshot name="screen-library" alt="The library with the Keymotion animation and two saved pictures, one marked On keyboard" />

The picture on the keyboard comes first, marked **On keyboard**. Each picture shows its frames, its loop length and its upload time, and has these buttons:

- **Upload** puts it on the GIF page. It asks first, like any upload.
- **Match lights** (the palette) designs key colors from it. See below.
- **Rename** (the pencil) changes its name.
- **Delete** (the bin) removes it. Click a second time to confirm.

With more than six pictures, you can show **All**, **Still** or **Animated** ones. With more than twelve, **Search library** finds a picture by name.

**Export settings** in [Settings](/keymotion/guide/settings/) saves the library in your backup file too.

## Match lights

**Match lights** builds a key color design from a picture. It appears after an upload or a save, and as the palette button on each picture in the library.

<Screenshot name="screen-match-lights" alt="The Match lights to the image window with the image's colors and the Gradient option selected" />

<Steps>

1. Click **Match lights**. The window shows the picture and its colors under **Image colors**.
2. Choose how the colors go on the keys:
   - **Gradient:** the picture's colors from left to right, in the order they appear.
   - **Single color:** the picture's main color on every key.
   - **Image:** the picture spread across the keys. Black areas stay dark.
3. Click **Open in Lighting**.

</Steps>

The design opens on the [Lighting](/keymotion/guide/lighting/) page as a per-key color design, and the keys show it. It replaces the per-key design there. The keyboard's saved lighting doesn't change until you save it.

## The factory animation and Keymotion's animation

A new keyboard shows an AJAZZ animation on its GIF page. Your first upload replaces it, and the keyboard can't give it back. Keymotion doesn't include AJAZZ's file.

- **If AJAZZ's official driver is installed on this PC,** Keymotion can copy the animation from it. Click **Back up from the official driver** on the Screen page or in the upload window. The library then holds the **AJAZZ factory animation**, marked **Factory**. Its **Restore** button puts it back on the GIF page, which takes about 5 minutes.
- **Without a backup,** the Screen page says so. Only AJAZZ's official driver can bring the animation back; see [If something goes wrong](/keymotion/help/recovery/).

Keymotion also has an animation of its own, ready in the library and marked **Comes with the app**. The "K" of Keymotion's logo is drawn stroke by stroke, then the name appears. It has 37 frames and takes about a minute and a half to upload. When there is no factory backup, the Screen page offers it, and **Show in library** takes you to it. Upload it like any other picture.

The factory backup and Keymotion's animation can't be renamed or deleted.

## The live screen

With the live screen on, Keymotion updates the GIF page by itself: a card before a meeting, a reminder, the focus timer's phases, or the song that is playing. Each card is an upload.

<Screenshot name="screen-live" alt="The Live screen tab with live screen on, the five sources in order and the daily upload limit" />

<Steps>

1. Open **Screen** and click the **Live screen** tab.
2. Turn on **Live screen**.
3. Switch the keyboard to the GIF page with **Fn+Home** to see the cards.

</Steps>

If the keyboard may still have its factory animation and there is no backup, Keymotion asks once before the first card: **Turn on live mode?** The first card erases the animation for good. Click **Turn on live mode** to go on, or **Cancel**.

The live screen runs while Keymotion runs, also when you close its window and it keeps running next to the clock (see [Settings](/keymotion/guide/settings/)). If you quit Keymotion, the last card stays on the screen.

### What shows on the screen

The **What shows on the screen?** list puts the sources in order. The highest source with something to show is on the screen. When it is done, the next one down takes over.

1. **Calendar:** a card before a meeting. Set it up on the [Calendar](/keymotion/guide/calendar/) page.
2. **Reminders:** a reminder's card, for the time you choose on the [Reminders](/keymotion/guide/reminders/) page.
3. **Focus timer:** focus and break cards while the timer runs. Set it up on the [Focus timer](/keymotion/guide/focus-timer/) page.
4. **Now playing:** the song playing in Spotify, a browser or another player, with its cover. Songs you skip within 4 seconds aren't uploaded.
5. **Your image:** the last picture you uploaded from the **Images** tab.

Each source shows what it is doing now, for example **On screen now**, **Waiting** or **Not playing**. The source on the screen is highlighted. The calendar, reminder and song sources each have a switch, so you can leave one out.

While the live screen is off, the screen changes only when you upload.

### Back to your own picture

After a card, your own picture comes back by itself:

- **A meeting's card** stays until the meeting starts, until 5 minutes after the start, or until the meeting ends, as you choose on the Calendar page.
- **A reminder's card** stays for 1, 2 or 5 minutes, as you choose on the Reminders page.
- **The focus timer** puts your picture back 15 seconds after a series ends, or shows a summary card, as you choose on the Focus timer page.
- **The song's card**: under **When the music stops**, choose **Back to your image in 5 min** or **Keep the card**.

If a source lower in the list still has something to show, it comes first. For example, the song's card comes back after a meeting's card.

The **Your image** row shows your picture and when it comes back. Click **Go back now** to put it back at once. Going back is an upload too.

When you turn off the live screen while a card is on the screen, Keymotion asks **Turn off live screen?** Choose **Restore previous image** or **Keep the card**. If you keep it, the tab offers **Restore previous image** again later.

## The daily upload limit

Every upload writes to the screen's memory, and that memory wears with use. Its exact lifespan isn't known. Memory like it usually lasts about 100,000 writes, so a few uploads a day are fine for years. To keep it that way, the live screen has a **Daily upload limit**:

| Choice | Live uploads |
|---|---|
| **Low (8)** | up to 8 a day, the easiest on the screen memory |
| **Balanced (24)** | up to 24 a day, enough for most uses |
| **High (48)** | up to 48 a day; wears the memory twice as fast as Balanced |

The bar under the choice shows how many uploads are available now and when the next one comes. Used uploads come back one by one through the day.

Your own uploads count toward the limit too, but they are never blocked.

When the limit is reached, a card waits. The Screen page says which card is waiting and that it isn't on the keyboard yet. On the **Live screen** tab, the waiting card has two buttons:

- **Send now** uploads it anyway. It still counts toward the limit.
- **Don't send** drops it. The keyboard keeps its current picture.

The focus timer and the song never take the last available upload; it is kept for a reminder or a meeting. A reminder's card never waits: if no upload is available, only the keys light up.

## If an upload stops partway

Keep the cable plugged in until an upload finishes. Other changes to the keyboard wait until then.

If the cable comes out, Keymotion quits or the computer shuts down during an upload, the GIF page holds only part of the picture and may look broken. Keymotion remembers this:

- The Screen page shows **The last upload didn't finish**, with an **Upload again** button. It uploads the same picture again from the start.
- In the library, that picture is marked **Incomplete** and its button reads **Upload again**.
- The next time Keymotion starts, a notice at the bottom right offers **Upload again** and **Go to Screen**.

If the picture is no longer in the library, upload any picture from the library to fix the page. See also [If something goes wrong](/keymotion/help/recovery/).

<Screenshot name="screen-incomplete-upload" alt="The Screen page with the notice The last upload didn't finish and the Upload again button" />

If you quit Keymotion while an upload runs, it shows **Saving to the keyboard** and closes by itself when the upload finishes. **Cancel** keeps Keymotion open. **Quit now** closes it at once and leaves the GIF page incomplete.
