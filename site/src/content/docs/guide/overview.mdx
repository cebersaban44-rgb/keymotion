The **Overview** is the first page while your keyboard is connected. It shows the keyboard's screen, how the keyboard is lit right now and where each tool stands. Every row leads to its own page.

<Screenshot name="overview" alt="The Overview over the USB cable: the keyboard's glass strip with its screen and knob, the keyboard drawing, and rows for lighting, focus timer, calendar, reminders, system gauges, the screen and the profile" />

## The glass strip

The page starts with the keyboard's glass strip, drawn like the one on the AK980 PRO: the screen on the left, the mountain picture printed on the glass, and the knob on the right.

- **The screen** shows the picture Keymotion put on the keyboard's GIF page. If Keymotion hasn't put one there, it shows the keyboard's clock page with the time now.
- **The knob** has an orange ring with five parts, one for each brightness level, lit up to the brightness Keymotion last saved with the lighting.

Under the strip is one line: the keyboard's name, the connection, such as **USB cable**, the firmware version when the keyboard reports it, and the layout and number of keys. On the right are the battery and **Knob: brightness 5/5**:

- **Over the USB cable**, the keyboard doesn't report its battery level. Keymotion shows instead whether it is charging: **Charging** or **Fully charged**. The keyboard lights its Fn key red while it charges, and Keymotion reads that light; the state appears once the light has stayed the same for about two seconds. Point at it to see the last Bluetooth reading, if there is one.
- **Over Bluetooth**, you see the battery level, such as **Battery 94%**. Point at it to see when it was read.

## The keyboard drawing

Over the USB cable, the drawing shows your keyboard's real light, live: Keymotion reads it about four times a second. Under the drawing it says **Showing your keyboard's lighting live** or **Keyboard lighting is off**. With an effect that lights only the keys you press, such as **Launch** or **Ripples**, the keys stay dark in between, and it says **Your keyboard lights up as you type**.

While Keymotion's window is in front, the keys you press light up in the drawing too (**Keys light up here as you press them.**). Fn and a few keys Windows keeps for itself, such as the Windows key and Print Screen, don't light up. The letters on the keys follow your Windows keyboard layout.

Over the 2.4 GHz receiver or Bluetooth, the drawing isn't live, and it says **The live view works over the USB cable only**.

## Status

Under **Status**, one row for each tool says what it is doing now; click a row to open its page. Apart from the focus timer's **Start** button and the switches of reminders and system gauges, nothing is changed from here.

- **Lighting** shows the effect Keymotion last saved to the keyboard, or **Per-key color**, its color and its brightness out of five; the colors are on the right. If Keymotion hasn't saved any lighting yet, it says **Not saved from the app yet**. While the system gauges are on, the keys show the gauges instead, and the row says so. You change the lighting and save it on the [Lighting](/keymotion/guide/lighting/) page.
- **Focus timer**, when it isn't running, sums up your sequence (how many blocks, how many minutes in all) and has a **Start** button. While it runs, you see focus or break, which block you are on, a bar and the time left. The row opens the [Focus timer](/keymotion/guide/focus-timer/) page.
- **Calendar** shows the next meeting, its time and how long until it starts, or **Happening now**. Without a calendar it says **No calendar added yet.**; with no meeting ahead, **No meetings in the next 24 hours.** The row opens the [Calendar](/keymotion/guide/calendar/) page.
- **Reminders** shows the next reminder with its time and how long until it, and on the right how many reminders are on, with a switch that turns all reminders on or off. With nothing coming up, it says so, for example **No upcoming reminders.**, **Reminders are off.** or **No reminders yet.** The row opens the [Reminders](/keymotion/guide/reminders/) page.
- **System gauges** has a switch that turns the gauges on or off. While on, it shows the **Processor**, **Memory** and **Graphics** loads that the keys display. While off, it says **Not shown on the keys.** and lists the gauges you chose. The row opens the [System gauges](/keymotion/guide/system-gauges/) page.
- **On the keyboard's screen now** names the picture on the keyboard's GIF page. With the live screen on, it says **Live screen on** and how many uploads are left; otherwise **Live screen off**. A card waiting for the daily upload limit is named after **Waiting:**. The row opens the [Screen](/keymotion/guide/screen/) page.
- **Profile** names the saved profile that matches what is on the keyboard, or says **Doesn't match a saved profile**. The row opens the [Profiles](/keymotion/guide/profiles/) page.

## Over the receiver or Bluetooth

When the keyboard is connected over Bluetooth, a bar under the keyboard says **Unlock every feature with the USB cable**: lighting, key mapping, macros and the screen can be set only over the USB cable. Plug in the cable and set the switch on the back to the middle; the page updates by itself.

With only the 2.4 GHz receiver plugged in, the bar says **2.4 GHz receiver plugged in** instead: Keymotion can't tell yet whether your keyboard is turned on and connected to the receiver, and settings need the USB cable here too. For the same reason, the dot next to **2.4 GHz receiver** under the glass strip is grey, not green.
