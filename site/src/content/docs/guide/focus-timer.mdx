The focus timer counts down focus and break blocks. The whole keyboard lights up in the block's color and the light recedes as the time runs out, so you can see how much is left without looking at the computer. If you like, the keyboard's screen shows a card for each block too.

Open **Focus timer** under **Tools** in the sidebar.

<Screenshot name="focus-timer" alt="The Focus timer page while a focus block runs, with the time left in a ring and the keyboard drawing beside it" />

<Aside type="note" title="Key lights and cards need the USB cable">
For the key lights and the screen cards, connect the cable and set the switch on the back of the keyboard to the middle. With the 2.4 GHz receiver or Bluetooth, the timer still runs on the page.
</Aside>

## Start, pause and end

<Steps>

1. Open **Focus timer**.
2. Click **Start**. The ring shows the time left, the phase (**Focus** or **Break**) and which block of the sequence is running.
3. Click **Pause** to stop the clock for a moment, and **Resume** to go on.
4. Click **Skip** to go to the next block, or **End** to end the series. Your saved lighting comes back on the keys.

</Steps>

You can also start the timer from the **Overview** page. When Keymotion keeps running after you close its window (see [Settings](/keymotion/guide/settings/)), the menu of its icon next to the clock shows the time left and can start, pause, skip and stop the timer.

After a series, **Last series** shows your focus blocks and minutes.

## Build your sequence

Under **Settings**, the **Sequence** row lists the blocks in the order they run.

- **Ready-made** gives you a start:
  - **25 / 5 classic** (the default): three 25-minute focus blocks with 5-minute breaks between them, then a 15-minute break.
  - **50 / 10 deep**: two 50-minute focus blocks, followed by a 10-minute and a 20-minute break.
  - **Start empty**: one 25-minute focus block.
- Change any block's length, from 1 to 180 minutes.
- **Add focus** and **Add break** add a block at the end. A sequence can have up to 16 blocks.
- Drag a block by the handle on its left to move it. **Remove block** (the cross) takes it out.
- **When the sequence ends** sets what happens after the last block: **Stop**, **2 times**, **3 times** or **Until I stop it**.

Changes apply at once, also to the block that is running.

## Key lights

The **Key lights** row is on by default. The keyboard lights up in each phase's color, and the light recedes as time runs out. Nothing is saved to the keyboard; your lighting comes back at the end.

- **Motion** sets how the light recedes: **Empties right to left**, **Empties left to right**, **Empties top to bottom**, **Empties toward the center** or **Fades slowly**.
- **Colors** has a color for **Focus** and one for **Break**. Click one to change it.
- **Brightness** goes from 1 to 5.
- **At phase start** plays a **Double blink** or a **Wave** when a new block begins.
- **Last-minute pulse** makes the keyboard breathe slowly in the last minute, so you notice without looking.

The drawing next to the ring shows how it looks. **Try on keyboard** plays focus, break and the end on your keyboard in 12 seconds.

While the timer runs, the load bars of the [system gauges](/keymotion/guide/system-gauges/) wait. Volume and microphone still show on their own keys.

## Cards on the screen

The **Keyboard screen** row sends a card to the keyboard's screen when each block starts. The card shows the phase, its length and what comes next. Cards go through the [live screen](/keymotion/guide/screen/) and count toward its daily upload limit.

<Screenshot name="focus-timer-cards" alt="The Keyboard screen row of the focus timer with the cards in order and the choice for the end of a series" />

- If the live screen is off, click **Turn on live screen**. Until then, the time shows only on the keys.
- **On screen, in order** shows the cards one series puts on the screen.
- A line says how many uploads one series uses and how many are available now. If they don't cover a full series, some cards may come late or be skipped; the keys still show the progress.

**When a series ends or stops**, choose what the screen shows:

- **Previous image:** if a song is playing, its card shows. Otherwise your own picture is uploaded again after 15 seconds. If you start again within that time, nothing is uploaded.
- **Summary card:** a card with your focus blocks and focus time. It changes when a song starts or another card comes.

If a card has to wait for the daily limit or the cable, the page says so and offers **Send now** and **Don't send**. Meeting and reminder cards come before the timer's cards.

## End-of-phase sound

Turn on **End-of-phase sound** to hear the Windows notification sound when a block ends. It plays only when the time runs out on its own, not when you skip or end. It is off by default.
