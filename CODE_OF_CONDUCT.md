# Code of conduct

Keymotion is a small project made for people who like their keyboards. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, experience or language. *Türkçe de yazabilirsiniz.*

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not programmers, and many write in their second language.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, politely.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Respect people's time: search before you post, and give the details others need to help you.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal information, and leave your own calendar addresses and personal data out of reports.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.
- Sharing material that breaks someone's rights, including the vendor's software or files.

## What happens

The maintainer ([@cebersaban44-rgb](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb)) may edit or remove posts, lock threads and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the post, or open a private report through [Security advisories](https://github.com/cebersaban44-rgb/keymotion/security/advisories/new) if it needs to stay private. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone speaking for the project elsewhere.
